Why service businesses run out of content

Most salons, coaches and local agents are great at the work and inconsistent at marketing. The problem is rarely strategy — it is the daily friction of thinking of a new angle, writing a caption and remembering the right holiday, on top of a full day of clients. Posting in bursts and then going quiet tells the algorithm, and your audience, that the account is inactive.

A pre-built calendar removes that daily decision. Instead of deciding whether to post and what to say from scratch, you open that day's row, add your name and a photo, and publish. The ideas rotate across seven content pillars — tips, social proof, behind the scenes, offers, education, trends and engagement — so the feed looks planned rather than repetitive.
